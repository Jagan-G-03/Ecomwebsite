Questions, answered

Good to know before you begin.

CareerBridge is a frontend demonstration. It does not connect to real employers or guarantee employment.

Is CareerBridge free to use?

Yes. Every feature in this demonstration is available in your browser at no cost.

How do I apply for jobs?

Open a job, choose Apply now, and complete the demo form. Records are stored locally and are not sent to employers.

Can freshers use CareerBridge?

Absolutely. Use the Fresher friendly filter, practice quizzes, and career guidance to get started.

How do I save a job?

Select the bookmark icon on any job card. Saved jobs remain after refresh in this browser.

Does CareerBridge guarantee employment?

No. Listings, outcomes, testimonials, and guidance are demonstration content only.
